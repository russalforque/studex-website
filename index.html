Private by design

Your data never leaves your phone.

Studex has no servers and no accounts. Everything is saved in a local database on your device, so it opens instantly and works the same with or without a connection.

  • No sign-up. Open the app and start, no email or password needed.
  • Offline first. Works in class, on the bus, anywhere without data.
  • No tracking. No ads, no analytics, nothing sent anywhere.
  • Safe changes. Deletes always ask first, and updates never lose your data.

Questions

Is Studex free?

Yes. Studex is free with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Do I need internet or an account?

No. Studex runs fully offline and doesn't ask you to sign up.

Where is my data stored?

In a local database on your phone. It isn't uploaded anywhere.

What happens if I uninstall the app?

Uninstalling removes the app's data from your phone. Backup and export are planned for a future update.

What's coming next?

Grades, attendance and notes are next, followed by budget analytics and backup / export.
